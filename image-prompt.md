# image-prompt.md

## 太空電梯設計文件插圖提示詞 (Image Prompts for Space Elevator Design Document)

### 圖像 1: 全局概覽 - 海基太空電梯與天環願景 (Global Overview - Sea-Based Space Elevator & GEO Ring Vision)
*   **插入位置：** `design-doc.md` -> `## 1. 系統總覽 (System Architecture)` 章節結束後，替換或增強現有的 ASCII 示意圖。
*   **提示詞：**
    ```
    Image of a futuristic space elevator (CVCSE) extending majestically from Earth's equator into space. The base is a massive, elegant floating platform on the open ocean (sea-based platform), with visible infrastructure for material transfer from the sea. Two main, slightly glowing tethers (cables) extend upwards, one showing ascending traffic and the other descending, conveying a continuous loop. The top station is a bright, intricate, multi-layered structure at geosynchronous orbit, adorned with vast solar panel arrays capturing sunlight. In the far distance, subtly integrated into the background, hints of a partially constructed, delicate orbital ring (GEO-Ring) around Earth's equator. The Earth below is vibrant with blue oceans and green landmasses, partially covered by thin clouds. The overall style should be optimistic, highly detailed, and scientifically plausible, with a focus on clean lines and advanced technology. Cinematic wide shot, high resolution, clear atmosphere.
    ```

### 圖像 2: 平流層基座 - 「真空項鍊」與再生工廠 (Stratospheric Base - "Vacuum Necklace" & Regeneration Factory)
*   **插入位置：** `design-doc.md` -> `### 第二階段：同步軌道「雙子星」對接與初始迴路建立` 之後，在「軌道站的張力抵銷機制」之前。
*   **提示詞：**
    ```
    Detailed cutaway illustration of the "Vacuum Necklace" stratospheric floating base, positioned at 50km altitude above a calm, deep blue ocean. The base is composed of numerous interconnected, transparent or translucent spherical "vacuum beads" (modules) forming a stable, vast platform. Inside the central part of the platform, show a bustling, highly automated, futuristic "Cable Regeneration Factory." Robotic arms with glowing tools are intricately working on fine, luminous strands of carbon nanotubes, demonstrating the unweaving, recycling, manufacturing, and weaving process. Depict a large, elegant hybrid airship (sea-to-stratosphere shuttle) gently docked at the base, unloading cargo containers. The entire base should glow softly with internal light, indicating constant activity, against a backdrop of Earth's curved horizon and the deep blue, clear sky of the stratosphere. High-tech, clean industrial design, showing scale and complexity.
    ```

### 圖像 3: 同步軌道站 - 雙絞盤與動態表面 (GEO Station - Dual Winches & Dynamic Surface)
*   **插入位置：** `design-doc.md` -> `### 第二階段：同步軌道「雙子星」對接與初始迴路建立` 中的「附註：絞盤的關鍵工程參數定量分析」之前。
*   **提示詞：**
    ```
    Close-up, cutaway technical illustration of one of the "Dual Counter-Rotating Winches" at the Geosynchronous Orbital Station. The winch is a massive, intricate cylindrical drum (approximately 70 meters in diameter), made of advanced, polished metallic alloys. Highlight the "dynamic surface" of the drum, showing it composed of numerous tiny, escalator-like micro-conveyor belts with individual grooves. Several dozen luminous, hair-thin carbon nanotube strands (representing the main cable) are tightly wrapped around the drum for about 20-30 turns. Show small robotic mechanisms actively adjusting the position of the cable wraps on the dynamic surface. The background shows the vacuum of space, with Earth visible as a distant, beautiful blue marble. The image should convey intricate mechanical precision, immense power, and futuristic engineering. Blue and white light accents.
    ```

### 圖像 4: 纜線再生 - 股線級編織流程 (Cable Regeneration - Woven Strand Process)
*   **插入位置：** `design-doc.md` -> `### 動態更換流程` (第三章) 之後。
*   **提示詞：**
    ```
    Detailed illustration of the "Woven Strand Dynamic Regeneration Process" occurring inside the Cable Regeneration Factory (located within the Stratospheric Base). Focus on a thick, continuously moving main space elevator cable, clearly shown to be composed of hundreds of thousands of individual, hair-thin, luminous carbon nanotube strands. Multiple highly articulated robotic arms with glowing tools are performing intricate tasks: one set gently unweaving an older, slightly dulled strand from the descending cable section, while another set simultaneously weaves a newly manufactured, bright, glowing strand into the ascending cable section. Show adjacent modules for recycling the old strand into raw materials and another module extruding the new, perfect strand. The factory interior is clean, futuristic, and filled with advanced robotics and glowing energy conduits.
    ```

*   **提示詞：**
    ```
    Dramatic illustration of a powerful, futuristic interplanetary cargo vessel being launched from the very top of the space elevator system, far beyond geosynchronous orbit.
    ```
